\noindent\textit{Note on this revision.} The original report (December 4, 2025) called the original protocol face recognition and read the pretrained models' collapse as brittleness under occlusion. This version makes three important changes: First, it identifies that protocol as image matching (Section~\ref{sec:original}); second, in an approximate re-creation of the pipeline, it traces most of SFace's collapse at 5 to 10\% occlusion to the face detector; and third, it removes what the result files do not support -- table cells that differ from the files, a column for a run of 1,000 images that stopped before writing results, and an area-under-the-curve table. The identification study in Sections~\ref{sec:design} to~\ref{sec:ablation} is new (November 2026).
