\section{Conclusion}

The original project's results measure matching an occluded image to its own clean copy, a task raw pixels already solve at small occlusions, and an approximate re-creation of the pipeline traces most of SFace's collapse there to the face detector, likely also for the other pretrained models. On one identity-disjoint split of CelebA-HQ with oracle alignment and synthetic occluders, a pretrained ArcFace identifies \N{r1_arc_s00}\% of probes clean and \N{r1_arc_s20}\% with 20\% of the crop covered. In a small CNN trained from scratch, which identifies only \N{cos_clean_lo} to \N{cos_clean_hi}\% clean, occlusion augmentation adds \N{P1_abs} points on the trained-on squares and \N{P2_abs} on average, unevenly, on unseen occluders, and costs \N{aug_s00_abs} on clean faces; the attention layer's \N{P3_abs}-point contrast comes from noise fills and the lower band, with no main effect detected on black squares with three seeds per configuration, and it costs \N{att_s00_abs} points on clean faces.
