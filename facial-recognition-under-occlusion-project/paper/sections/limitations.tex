\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Oracle alignment.} Faces are found and aligned on the clean image, then occluded, so the study is likely an upper bound for a real pipeline, whose detector, in the CPU re-creation for SFace (Section~\ref{sec:original}), stops finding the face first. All \N{n_test_identities} test identities survived alignment.
\item \textit{Synthetic occluders.} The occluders are squares, bands, and flat or noise fills drawn on aligned crops; there are no masks, sunglasses, or hands.
\item \textit{One dataset.} CelebA-HQ holds mostly frontal, high-quality photos of celebrities. The identity labels are CelebA-Dialog's, read through an image map built by thumbnail matching and checked only as in Section~\ref{sec:design}. There is one identity split. CelebA-HQ also holds a few near-duplicate pictures, which the split does not remove. The \N{nc_n} probes most similar to their gallery image (ArcFace R50 cosine at least \N{nc_cos}; not checked by eye for near-copies) hold \N{nc_orig_hits} of the original model's \N{nc_orig_hits_all} clean hits; without them, it identifies \N{nc_r1_orig_without}\%, raw crop pixels \N{nc_r1_pxc_without}\%, and ArcFace R50 \N{nc_r1_arc_without}\%.
\item \textit{Pretrained training data.} The pretrained models were trained on web-collected face datasets that may include some of these celebrities; this was not checked, and their clean scores may be higher for that reason.
\item \textit{Seeds.} Intervals resample test identities and training seeds together, and with \N{n_seeds} seeds per configuration, the seed part is coarse. The bootstrap resamples probes by identity but keeps the \N{n_gallery}-image gallery fixed. No result here supports a null effect.
\item \textit{Settings.} There was no validation split, and the trained CNNs may be far from what tuning would give.
\item \textit{Two coverage axes.} Crop coverage (the study) and frame coverage (the original runs) are different axes, and no number is compared across them.
\item \textit{The original runs.} These are single runs, with one unseeded sample per $N$, a different sample for each $N$, and no intervals.
\item \textit{Scope of the ablation.} The ablation covers one small architecture, trained from scratch at one scale; the optional stage that runs DeepFace's six models on the aligned crops was not run, so nothing here speaks to VGG-Face, either FaceNet, OpenFace, or DeepFace's ArcFace under alignment.
\end{itemize}
