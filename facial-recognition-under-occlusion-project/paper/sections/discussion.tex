\section{Discussion}

The original project asked the right question and left a reproducible model: the saved weights still give its $N=50$, 100, and 200 curves within a few images. What the protocol hid is that matching an occluded image to its own clean copy is easy wherever enough of the image is left, and that the comparison it made was between a model trained on exactly that task, comparing whole frames, and pretrained recognizers whose detector, for SFace, stopped finding the face. Once the probe is a different photo (and, for the crop models, the face is aligned before it is covered), the order reverses: the saved model identifies \N{r1_orig_f00}\% of clean probes, a pretrained ArcFace R50 (not the original runs' ArcFace) identifies \N{r1_arc_s00}\%, and the project's architecture, retrained here from scratch, identifies \N{cos_clean_lo} to \N{cos_clean_hi}\%.

Within that small network, occlusion augmentation is a trade. It buys about \N{P1_abs} points on the occluder it trains with and \N{P2_abs} on average on others, and pays \N{aug_s00_abs} points on clean faces, so whether it helps depends on how often the faces a system sees are covered. The attention layer buys little on black squares at this scale and costs \N{att_s00_abs} points on clean faces; its measured benefit is under noise fills, where the augmented network without it has rank-1 at or below chance, and on the lower band in the network trained without augmentation (with augmentation, it cost \N{sc_attA_low_abs} points there). That is narrower than the original report's reading, that attention steers the network toward the uncovered parts of the face, and the attention weights themselves cannot settle it, for the reason given in Section~\ref{sec:ablation}.

For the pretrained models and these occluders, a band across the eyes or the lower face, or a square away from the center, costs far less than a centered square of similar size. And for these models, at a threshold fixed on clean faces and with a clean gallery image, occlusion raises the rate of rejecting the right person, not of admitting the wrong one: it pushes fewer impostor pairs, as well as fewer genuine ones, above the threshold, so the actual false accept rate falls while the true accept rate drops.
