# Literature

The original project's reading list. The papers are not stored in the repository; each title links to the paper. The paper's references are in [`../paper/sections/references.tex`](../paper/sections/references.tex).

- Amos, B., Ludwiczuk, B., and Satyanarayanan, M. (2016). [OpenFace: A general-purpose face recognition library with mobile applications](http://reports-archive.adm.cs.cmu.edu/anon/2016/CMU-CS-16-118.pdf). Technical Report CMU-CS-16-118, School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University.
- Deng, J., Guo, J., An, X., Zhu, Z., and Zafeiriou, S. (2021). [Masked Face Recognition Challenge: The InsightFace Track Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08191). ICCV Workshops.
- Ge, S., Li, J., Ye, Q., and Luo, Z. (2017). [Detecting Masked Faces in the Wild with LLE-CNNs](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2017/html/Ge_Detecting_Masked_Faces_CVPR_2017_paper.html). CVPR.
- Parkhi, O. M., Vedaldi, A., and Zisserman, A. (2015). [Deep Face Recognition](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/2015/Parkhi15/). BMVC.
- Paszke, A., Gross, S., Massa, F., Lerer, A., et al. (2019). [PyTorch: An Imperative Style, High-Performance Deep Learning Library](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01703). NeurIPS.
- Schroff, F., Kalenichenko, D., and Philbin, J. (2015). [FaceNet: A Unified Embedding for Face Recognition and Clustering](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.03832). CVPR.
- Serengil, S. I., and Ozpinar, A. (2020). [LightFace: A Hybrid Deep Face Recognition Framework](https://doi.org/10.1109/ASYU50717.2020.9259802). Innovations in Intelligent Systems and Applications Conference (ASYU), IEEE.
- Vaswani, A., Shazeer, N., Parmar, N., Uszkoreit, J., et al. (2017). [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762). NeurIPS.
- Woo, S., Park, J., Lee, J.-Y., and Kweon, I. S. (2018). [CBAM: Convolutional Block Attention Module](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06521). ECCV.

## Data

- Liu, Z., Luo, P., Wang, X., and Tang, X. (2015). [Deep Learning Face Attributes in the Wild](https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/projects/CelebA.html). ICCV. The CelebA dataset, for non-commercial research use only.
- Karras, T., Aila, T., Laine, S., and Lehtinen, J. (2018). [Progressive Growing of GANs for Improved Quality, Stability, and Variation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10196). ICLR. The source of CelebA-HQ.
